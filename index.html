Rafisol

A multipurpose server bot - moderation, logging, automod, role menus and more, all configured from one settings file.

Open the Settings Editor Add to Discord

Moderation

Ban, softban, kick, timeout, mute, warn, purge, lockdown and slowmode. Temporary actions, numbered cases, and warnings that escalate automatically.

Logging

Message edits and deletes, voice activity, joins and leaves, and audit-log style server changes - who did what, posted through webhooks.

Automod + Honeypot

Word, invite, link, mention, spam and caps filters, plus a "do not type here" channel that softbans hacked accounts before they spam everywhere.

Roles

Button and dropdown role menus, self-assignable roles, booster custom roles, autoroles, role persist, and per-command permissions.

Join / Leave

Welcome and goodbye messages with embeds and placeholders, plus an optional welcome DM.

Webhook Posts

Write your rules and info posts once, post them with a custom name and avatar, and edit them in place later.

Setting it up

  1. Run /config export in your server.
  2. Open the Settings Editor and import the file.
  3. Change what you like, export, and upload it with /config import.
